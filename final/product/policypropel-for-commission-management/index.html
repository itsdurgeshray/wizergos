Frequently asked questions

How quickly can commission structures be updated?

EOM allows commission grids and intermediary-level exceptions to be updated within hours or days, enabling faster response to market needs.

How does the system ensure governance?

All changes go through structured approval workflows with impact analysis and are recorded in detailed audit logs.

Can agents see how their commissions are calculated?

Yes. Agents can see reports with detailed breakdown of commissions for each policy and transaction.

Does EOM support flexible payout cycles?

Yes. Organizations can enable daily, weekly, or periodic payouts based on business requirements.

How are broker invoices managed?

EOM includes an integrated invoice generation and approval system for brokers, streamlining financial workflows.

Does EOM replace existing finance systems?

No. It integrates with your existing systems and acts as the commission processing and control layer.
