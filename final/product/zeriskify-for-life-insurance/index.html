The Challenge

Insurance underwriting requires detailed medical interviews before a policy can be issued. However, coordinating these interviews with doctors often slows down the process and causes applicants to drop off before completing the underwriting journey.

01

Delays in Medical Interview Scheduling

Applicants often wait several days for doctor availability to complete required underwriting interviews.

02

Applicant Drop-Off During the Interview Stage

Many applicants lose momentum or choose competing insurers while waiting for interview scheduling.

03

Inconsistent Capture of Medical Information

Manual interviews may miss important follow-up questions or collect incomplete medical disclosures.

04

Manual Interview Coordination Effort

Underwriting teams spend significant time arranging interviews and tracking their completion before evaluation can begin.

Solution
01

Instant Applicant Interviews

Applicants can complete underwriting interviews immediately after submitting their application, without waiting for doctor availability or scheduled calls. This helps reduce delays and keeps applicants engaged in the process.

02

Intelligent & Verified AI Interviews

The AI voice bot conducts structured medical interviews through conversational interaction, dynamically asking follow-up questions based on applicant responses. Identity verification ensures the person completing the interview is the actual applicant, helping prevent fraudulent disclosures.

03

Recorded & Underwriting-Ready Reports

Each interview is securely recorded and automatically converted into structured summaries and transcripts. Underwriters and doctors can quickly review the information, creating a reliable record that supports accurate risk assessment and helps minimize future claim disputes.

Frequently asked questions

Do applicants need to schedule a call with a doctor for the interview?

No. Applicants can complete the underwriting interview instantly through the AI interaction without waiting for doctor availability.

Is the applicant’s information secure?

Yes. All interview responses and disclosures are securely stored and accessible only to authorized underwriting teams.

How long does the underwriting interview take?

Most interviews can be completed within a few minutes, depending on the applicant’s responses and medical disclosures.

What happens after the interview is completed?

The system automatically generates structured summaries and transcripts that underwriting teams can review for evaluation.

Can applicants complete the interview anytime?

Yes. Applicants can take the interview at their convenience without waiting for scheduled calls.

Does this replace underwriting decisions?

No. The platform only conducts and organizes interviews. Final underwriting decisions remain with the underwriting team.
