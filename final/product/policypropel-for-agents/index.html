Frequently asked questions

Does the AI generate answers outside of approved documents?

No. Responses are grounded strictly in connected, approved internal documents. Source references are included to maintain compliance confidence.

How does the platform access performance and incentive data?

The platform integrates securely with your existing commission, RnR, and transaction systems to surface real-time goal and incentive visibility within the agent interface.

Is this replacing existing CRM or policy systems?

No. The assistant operates as an intelligence layer on top of your existing systems, enhancing access and visibility rather than replacing core infrastructure.

Can agents use it during live calls?

Yes. Agents can interact via chat or voice during live customer conversations without disrupting workflow.

Do agents need training to use this?

No extensive training is required. Agents can ask questions in plain language, just like they would speak to a colleague. Most teams are productive from day one.

Are answers consistent across all agents and channels?

Yes. All users access the same approved knowledge base, ensuring consistent answers across calls and chat.
