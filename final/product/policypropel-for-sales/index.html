Frequently asked questions

Does the platform use external data sources?

No. All insights and responses are based strictly on approved internal documents and connected performance data.

Can sales teams see product-level knowledge challenges?

Yes. The platform highlights recurring queries and engagement patterns across specific products.

Does this replace our CRM or performance systems?

No. The platform operates as an intelligence layer on top of your existing systems.

Does this require changes to our existing sales workflows?

No. The platform enhances visibility and preparation without altering core sales processes.

Will this disrupt how agents currently access documents?

No. It connects to approved documentation sources and improves access without replacing them.
