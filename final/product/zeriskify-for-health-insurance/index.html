The Challenge

Medical underwriting requires detailed health interviews. However, coordinating these interviews with doctors delays the process and leads to applicant drop-offs.

01

Long Wait for Medical Interviews

Applicants often wait days for doctor availability to complete underwriting interviews.

02

Applicant Drop-Off During Interviews

Many applicants lose interest or move to competitors while waiting for interview scheduling.

03

Inconsistent Medical Data Collection

Manual interviews can miss follow-up questions or capture incomplete medical disclosures.

04

Manual Interview Coordination

Underwriting teams spend time scheduling and tracking interviews before evaluation can begin.

Solution
01

Instant Applicant Interviews

Applicants can complete medical underwriting interviews immediately after submitting their application. There is no need to wait for doctor availability or scheduled tele-interviews, reducing delays and preventing applicant drop-offs.

02

Intelligent & Verified AI Interviews

The AI bot conducts structured medical interviews through conversational voice interaction, dynamically asking follow-up questions based on applicant responses. Identity verification ensures the person completing the interview is the actual applicant, helping prevent fraudulent disclosures.

03

Recorded & Underwriting-Ready Reports

Every interview is securely recorded and automatically converted into structured summaries and transcripts. Underwriters and doctors can review the interview instantly, creating a verifiable record that supports accurate risk assessment and helps reduce future claim disputes.

Frequently asked questions

Do applicants need to wait for a medical reviewer to start the interview?

No. Applicants can start and complete the underwriting interview instantly through the AI interaction without waiting for reviewer availability.

Is my medical information safe?

Yes. All medical disclosures and interview responses are securely stored and accessible only to authorized underwriting teams.

What type of health information will be asked in the interview?

The interview captures details about medical history, current health conditions, treatments, and lifestyle factors relevant to health insurance underwriting.

How long does the health underwriting interview take?

Most interviews take only a few minutes, depending on the applicant’s medical history and responses.

What happens after the interview is completed?

The system automatically generates structured summaries and transcripts that underwriting teams can review for eligibility evaluation.

Does this replace the underwriting decision process?

No. The platform only conducts and organizes interviews. Final underwriting decisions are still made by the underwriting team.
