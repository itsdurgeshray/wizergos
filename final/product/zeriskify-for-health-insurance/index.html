FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Zeriskify help with?

It supports medical underwriting interviews by helping collect applicant disclosures and organize information for review.

Does Zeriskify make the final underwriting decision?

No. The authorized underwriting team remains responsible for the final decision.

Can applicants complete interviews without a scheduled doctor call?

The published product description presents guided AI interviews as a way to reduce dependence on doctor scheduling.

Which languages are available?

Availability depends on product configuration; contact the team for the current list.
