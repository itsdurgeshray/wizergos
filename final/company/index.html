About Us

Headquartered in Bangalore, India, Wizergos is a low code application platform. Using its platform, the low code development company streamlines the process of application development for enterprises across major industries, notably in BFSI, Insurance, Banking, Healthcare, Pharma, Manufacturing, and Retail.

The Wizergos Platform allows businesses to deploy sophisticated and agile enterprise-grade mobile and web applications to accelerate productivity and growth.

Led by a team of industry veterans, Wizergos strives ahead on its mission to drive the digital transformation via the adoption of low code technology by providing futuristic and innovative solutions to clients.

Our Story

10XEnterprise Applications have been built at 10X the speed of conventional Application development

We believe that enterprise-grade software has been difficult and expensive to build rendering it inaccessible for a majority of organizations. We are on a mission to change this forever- through Low-Code!

In the new digital world all businesses will be Software Businesses: Software will be core to success and businesses need to take control. We want to play our role in this transformation.

We want to enable the thinkers and visionaries: the business and IT teams with a vision of a better digital business to achieve their goals faster, at lower cost, lower project risks.

We believe that modern enterprise applications need to be built on a robust platform, in lightning speed to meet the changing market demands. For too long mid to large businesses have taken the slow, expensive, and risky path of depending on IT Services organizations for all their technology needs.

Using the Wizergos Platform, Enterprise Applications have been built at 10X the speed of conventional Application development, making it possible to build Apps in weeks instead of months or years.
