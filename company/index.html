WHO WE ARE

Making technology more accessible.

Good ideas deserve a simpler path to becoming real applications.

Based in Bengaluru, Wizergos is a low-code application platform that helps enterprises build mobile and web applications across industries, including insurance, banking, healthcare, pharma, manufacturing, and retail.

Our mission is to make enterprise software easier to build, giving business and IT teams a faster way to bring their ideas to life.

We believe businesses should have the flexibility to innovate, adapt, and create solutions that meet their evolving needs. Through our platform, we help teams turn their vision into enterprise-grade applications with less development effort and greater agility.

SMShibabrata MondalCo-Founder & CEO | IIT Kharagpur alumnus
More about our mission

Enterprise software has traditionally been expensive and time-consuming to develop. We believe businesses should have the tools to shape their own digital future.

Led by industry veterans, Wizergos empowers teams to transform ideas into applications with greater speed, flexibility, and efficiency.

Our platform supports the creation of enterprise-grade mobile and web applications that help businesses improve productivity and respond to changing needs.

WHAT DRIVES US

Innovation starts with people.

We believe meaningful innovation happens when people have the freedom to think differently, the tools to bring ideas to life, and the opportunity to work together.

Ideas can come from anyone.

We value the merit of an idea, wherever it comes from. We encourage people to ask questions, share perspectives, and challenge the usual way of doing things.

Take ownership. Take pride.

We care about the things we build and the problems we solve. Ownership means bringing thought, initiative, and accountability to our work.

Different perspectives. Shared ambition.

We bring together different experiences, ideas, and traditions. We believe these differences make us stronger and help us grow together.
